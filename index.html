GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Are these QR codes really free?+

Yes. This noncommercial website is free to use, with no paid features or accounts.

Will the QR codes ever expire?+

Static QR codes have no built-in expiration date. The linked webpage or resource must still exist for the link to work.

Can I change the link after downloading?+

No. These are static QR codes; the destination is encoded inside the image. To use a different link, make a new code. A redirect URL you control can provide flexibility.

Do you store my data?+

No. This generator processes QR content in your browser and includes no tracking scripts or QR generation service. The website host may still log normal website requests.
